How Advising Works

It starts with an open, one-on-one conversation with a member of our team. We listen first: what you want from practice, where you are in life, and what kind of residential setting interests you. Then we connect you to resources and help you discern which factors matter most to you.

If residential training fits, we recommend one or several practice centers: only centers we know to be trustworthy and compatible with your background, aims, and interests, drawing on our staff and advisory board.

Advising is offered by donation, like everything we do.

Not sure you’re ready? That’s what the conversation is for.
