What to expect

Retreats, immersions & workshops

Retreat

Residential, mostly silent

Retreats at Dharma Gates are residential practice experiences rooted in lineage-based Dharma transmission and held within a strong supportive container. Most retreats are held in noble silence — refraining from unnecessary talking, including social courtesies, as well as from reading, writing, and technology use unless explicitly invited by the teacher. Retreats are practice-heavy and experiential, rather than conceptual or lecture-based. All retreats are led by Dharma teachers with permission to teach from within their lineage.

Immersion

Live the rhythm of a community

Immersions are multi-day experiences designed to introduce participants to the life and flow of a monastic or lineage-based community. Periods of silence may be included, but silence is not central. Immersions often feature more conceptual learning, discussion, and interactive elements.

Workshop

Shorter, teaching-forward

Workshops are shorter, practice-oriented learning experiences designed to introduce participants to specific aspects of Dharma practice. They are generally not silent. Workshops are primarily talk and teaching heavy, supplemented by experiential exercises and educational activities.

Ongoing

Practice between retreats

Dharma Office Hours monthly · online

Dharma Office Hours is a monthly online space to practice with sangha and bring your questions. The format is simple: meditate together, then group Q&A. It's here for you if you're looking for help troubleshooting your meditation practice, accountability and encouragement from community, support integrating your retreat experience into daily life, or a way to learn and engage with the Dharma before a retreat.

Cost

Every program is a gift

Inspired by the Buddha's teachings on sila (morality) and his emphasis on generosity as a spiritual practice, we have chosen not to require any charges or suggest specific payment amounts for our retreats. We fully cover all expenses related to the retreat, including meals, housing, rental costs, and any teacher fees. Rather than offering a sliding scale or suggested donations, we offer all retreats and programs as a gift, freely accessible to all participants, without obligation of payment.
