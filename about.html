About Dharma Gates

Dharma Gates is a grassroots nonprofit dedicated to opening pathways into formal meditation practice for young adults. We connect young practitioners to a variety of Buddhist teachers and practice centers, working to eliminate conceptual, logistical, and financial barriers to practice. We foster connections through which practitioners can cultivate compassion, resilience, and clarity to meet the unique challenges of our times.

After a spiritual crisis his freshman year of college, Aaron encountered contemplative practice in 2014 through Mysore-style Ashtanga yoga with his first teacher, Angela Jamison. He encountered formal Buddhist practice at Dai Bosatsu Zendo in 2017 and has been serving as Dharma Gates' Executive Director since graduating in 2019. Since then, his practice has taken him across all three major Buddhist lineages — including three and a half years in Zen practice communities, five separate month-long silent retreats in the tradition of Pa Auk Sayadaw, and in 2023, a connection to his root tradition in the Dudjom Tersar lineage of the Nyingma. Aaron's approach honors both the importance of integration and psychological healing and the depths of traditional lineage practice and the possibilities of liberation contained therein. To him, Dharma Gates is an expression of a vow to translate the classical Buddhist path into new contexts for new audiences.

Dana: Everything Is a Gift

Dharma Gates events are offered within the practice of Dana (दान), the Sanskrit/Pali word meaning generosity or giving. Rather than operating on a fee structure, we instead ask you to engage with us in a relationship of reciprocity, giving what feels aligned with your circumstances and values. If these experiences are precious to you, we invite you to donate in a way that reflects that. Doing so will breathe life into our efforts, helping us to continue creating beautiful, accessible practice opportunities for young people for years to come. Donation is possible upon registration, or by visiting this link.

Inspired by the Buddha's teachings on sila (morality) and his emphasis on generosity as a spiritual practice, we have chosen not to require any charges or suggest specific payment amounts for our retreats. We fully cover all expenses related to the retreat, including meals, housing, rental costs, and any teacher fees. Rather than offering a sliding scale or suggested donations, we offer all retreats and programs as a gift, freely accessible to all participants, without obligation of payment. Any donation—no matter how small—helps us to continue to offer these retreats to future participants. Depending on one's life situation, this donation could be $3 or $3,000.

Inclusion

Our goal at Dharma Gates is to make sure deep meditation practice is accessible to any young person who is called towards it. We welcome people of all cultural, ethnic, and religious backgrounds. We welcome people of any racial identity, ethnic identity, ability, neurodiversity, socio-economic status, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, age, education, country of origin, veteran's status, religion, and level or lineage of practice. We encourage you to reach out to us for any accommodations that would be beneficial for you.
